# Renewable Energy Development in the Eastern Cape

A GeoDev Lab Africa project investigating the spatial distribution and development history of renewable energy in the Eastern Cape, South Africa.

## Project Question

> What can the spatial distribution and history of renewable energy development tell us about where and how renewable energy is actually developing in the Eastern Cape, South Africa?

## Study Area

Eastern Cape, South Africa.

## Project Overview

The project investigates renewable energy development using spatial and temporal data on renewable energy projects, renewable resources, electricity infrastructure and municipal boundaries.

The work is being developed into an **Eastern Cape Renewable Energy Opportunity & Development Atlas**.

# Month 1 Result

The analysis shows that renewable-energy projects are **spatially unevenly distributed across the Eastern Cape**. Some municipalities contain substantially more recorded renewable-energy projects than others, indicating spatial concentration rather than an even distribution across the province.

This provides a direct answer to the project's spatial-distribution question: **renewable-energy development is concentrated in particular municipalities rather than being uniformly distributed across the Eastern Cape.**
